**One writer:** a single builder owns S2–S6, one PR per stage, serially. S0 goes to a different
builder and may start now. No other task may edit `native/engine2` or `native/control` while S2–S5 are open. `native/sidecar`
is written by M1 only in S5: the sidecar tasks of M0 (T0.13a) and M2 (E1a) merge before S5a opens
or wait until S5b is merged; M0 work in `bench/` and `facts/` continues in parallel.

**Contract-review:** the lead posts the review request (template in `docs/workflow/README.md`)
with base and head SHAs and the quoted invariants, sets `needs/fable`, and the owner sends
`review #<PR>` to a Claude session. Nothing High means the lead merges.
