## Invariants
- **X1** Golden 24 identical on three platforms on R1 and PL1; instruction counts within 1 %.
- **X2** Plan and compiler output are pure functions of their inputs: two runs on two
  platforms give identical bytes. No timestamp, path, host name or map-order dependence.
- **X3** The compiler never fetches, never calls an AI, and never drops a module: every
  refusal names the module and the rule.
- **X4** CP2 compares bit for bit (`0013` Q4). A tolerance is not available to the lead or
  the deputy.
- **X5** Build only. No flashing, no device, no LAN. Third-party code is pinned by hash with
  its licence recorded.
- **X6** Facts files are neutral and carry commit, machine and date.

## Acceptance
1. S1–S6 filed. S1 shows equal plan-order hashes on three platforms, or the defect is listed.
2. A1 and A2 build in CI; the artefact sizes are filed.
3. R1 and PL1 merged under X1; the engine has one planner and one copy of the host arithmetic.
4. CP1 accepts at least three bundled patches made only of portable modules and refuses one
   patch for each of rules 1–3 with the expected message. CP2 is green for the accepted ones.
5. The report lists what is unverified on hardware. The owner's device run is recorded when
   it arrives and does not hold acceptance.

## Escalate
A plan-order difference between platforms; R1 costing more than 1 %; CP2 not bit-identical;
a board fact that makes the first board unusable (`0013` A4); two failed correction rounds.
